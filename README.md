Typing_car is a game where players race by typing different words and the faster you type the faster you go. On the home screen, there are  3 options shop, achievements, or race now. If you press race now, you race with other players that pressed race now around the time you pressed or if nobody pressed at the same time, the person will race computer also there can only be 5 people playing at a time. Depending on the speed, who ever gets first, gets $2300 and decreases by a $100 dollars. If you go to shop, there will be many options of cars like Bugatti's, Lamborghini's, Porsche, formula one race car, Mclaren, Ferrari, and Ford Mustang as the free beginner car. The Porsche costs $560,000 the Ferrari costs $850,000 and the Mclaren costs $1,000,000 and the Lamborghini costs $1,400,000 also the Bugatti costs $1,600,000 and the Formula one race car costs $2,000,000. Last but not least when you go into achievements, it opens a screen where you see different achievements like you beat your high score of WPM, WPM stands for words per minute and once you beat it you get $500,000.
